Camera Topics (from camera/camera_launch/config/cameras.yaml)
=============================================================

Each camera publishes under /<namespace>/<name>/...
Depth is disabled on all cameras (color only).
Raw comes from the RealSense node; compressed comes from a separate
image_transport republish process per camera.

WORLD camera
  Raw color        : /camera/world/color/image_raw
  Compressed color : /camera/world/color/image_raw/compressed
  Camera info      : /camera/world/color/camera_info

RIGHT camera
  Raw color        : /right/camera/color/image_raw
  Compressed color : /right/camera/color/image_raw/compressed
  Camera info      : /right/camera/color/camera_info

LEFT camera
  Raw color        : /left/camera/color/image_raw
  Compressed color : /left/camera/color/image_raw/compressed
  Camera info      : /left/camera/color/camera_info

Data-collection config (ui/Clients_UI/daksha_data_collection/config/config.yaml)
uses the compressed feeds:
  world : /camera/world/color/image_raw/compressed
  left  : /left/camera/color/image_raw/compressed
  right : /right/camera/color/image_raw/compressed


ros2 launch aruco_hand_eye daksha_hand_eye.launch.py
